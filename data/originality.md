# Originality Rules

The block-building sandbox *genre* is open to anyone. Game mechanics like digging, placing blocks, crafting, and procedural worlds generally aren't protected by copyright. What *is* protected is a specific game's **expression** (art, sounds, music, code, text, character designs) and its **trademarks** (names, logos, distinctive branding).

These rules keep this project clearly its own. *(This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.)*

## Never

- **No names or branding from other games.** No "Minecraft", no "-craft" suffix, no "Creeper", "Enderman", "Steve", "Alex", "Redstone", "Nether", "The End", "Ender", "Notch", etc.
- **No copied assets.** No textures, sounds, music, models, or fonts taken or traced from another game, including "texture packs" or "resource packs".
- **No lookalike characters or creatures.** No green exploding things, no tall black teleporters, no blocky-guy-in-a-teal-shirt. If a creature is instantly recognisable as someone else's, redesign it.
- **No copied code.** Don't decompile or port another game's code. Open-source libraries are fine when their license allows it; credit them.
- **No logo or UI cloning.** No stone-textured title in that font, no exact copies of the inventory screen, hotbar art, or splash-text style.
- **Never imply affiliation.** We are not a "Minecraft clone", "Minecraft for browser", or "free Minecraft" in any public text.

## Always

- **Our own names** for every block, item, creature, biome, and dimension. Keep a naming doc.
- **Our own art.** Hand-made or procedurally generated textures, with our own palette and resolution choices.
- **Our own sound.** Recorded, synthesised, or properly licensed (CC0 / CC-BY with credit).
- **A signature twist.** At least one core mechanic that makes this game feel different, not just reskinned.
- **Credits file.** Every third-party library and asset with its license.
- **Describe by genre.** "Voxel sandbox", "block-building survival game", "open-world builder".

## Gray areas: when in doubt, change it

| Fine | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Cube-shaped blocks | Exact copies of iconic block textures |
| A crafting system | A 3×3 grid with identical recipes |
| Hostile night creatures | Recognisable lookalikes of famous mobs |
| A hell-like dimension | Calling it "the Nether" or copying its look |
| A sword, a pickaxe | Pixel-identical item sprites |

If a playtester says *"oh, that's the ___ from ___"*, it goes on the redesign list.
