# Devlog #1: First playable build

Hollowstone is playable. **[Play it in your browser →](https://vibratingcode.github.io/hollowstone/)**

![A claybrick cottage at the edge of a Duskwood forest](assets/projects/hollowstone/build.jpg)

## What's in

- **An endless world.** Chunks of 16×16×128 blocks stream in around you. Pick a seed and you'll always get the same world back.
- **Seven biomes.** Meadows, violet **Duskwood** forests, dunes, frostlands, peaks, shores and ocean.
- **The hollows.** Deep underground, huge caverns open up, studded with **Lumen** crystals. Lumen gives off real light that floods across the cave walls in teal, so in the dark caves the crystals are how you find your way.
- **Building.** Break, place and pick blocks from a 9-slot hotbar, or press **E** for the full block picker.
- **19 original blocks**: Turf, Loam, Slate, Siltsand, Barkwood, Canopy, Timber, Rubble, Basalt, Emberore, Lumen, Claybrick, Glass, Snowpack, Duskwood and more. Every texture is painted by code.
- **Movement.** Walk, sprint, sneak (you won't fall off edges), swim, or double-tap Space to fly.
- **Saves.** Worlds save automatically in your browser, and you can keep as many as you like.

![Lumen crystals lighting a hollow](assets/projects/hollowstone/hollow.jpg)

## Under the hood

The game is plain JavaScript modules plus three.js, with no build step, so it deploys straight to GitHub Pages. The engine has no dependencies and is covered by tests: noise, world generation, meshing, raycasting and player physics.

The lighting combines three things: per-vertex **ambient occlusion**, a **sky-light** heightmap (caves go dark), and a **flood-fill block light** from Lumen that crosses chunk borders.
