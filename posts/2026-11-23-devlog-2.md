# Devlog #2: The Light Update

Hollowstone 0.2 is all about light. **[Play it →](https://vibratingcode.github.io/hollowstone/)**

![Morning in Hollowstone](assets/projects/hollowstone/v02-day.jpg)

## The sky moves now

The sun rises, crosses the sky and sets, and then the moon and stars come out. The sky shader shifts from deep night blue, through dusky pink at dawn, to clear day, with a warm glow around the sun as it nears the horizon. The fog follows the sky so the horizon always blends in.

![Sunset through Duskwood](assets/projects/hollowstone/v02-sunset.jpg)

## Real shadows

The sun (or the moon at night) casts real-time shadows from terrain, trees and anything you build. Leaves cast leaf-shaped shadows, gaps and all.

## Living world

- **Water** ripples, reflects the sky and throws glints of sunlight.
- **Wind** sways the leaves and the new plants.
- **Flora:** Bellgrass in the meadows, Emberbloom flowers, Frostbells in the snow, and **Glowcaps**, luminous mushrooms that grow in Duskwood and on hollow floors.
- **Fireflies** come out over meadows and forests at night.

![Night at the cottage](assets/projects/hollowstone/v02-night.jpg)

## Glow

Everything renders in HDR with bloom and ACES tone mapping, so Lumen crystals, Glowcaps and the sun actually shine. In the hollows, teal motes of light drift through the dark.

![A hollow](assets/projects/hollowstone/v02-hollow.jpg)

## Feel

Your held block now sits in the corner of the screen and swings when you break or place. The camera bobs as you walk, the view widens when you sprint, and broken blocks burst into little pieces.

## Under the hood

- Chunk meshing moved to **Web Workers**, so loading new areas doesn't stutter.
- A new terrain material: three.js Lambert lighting with injected shader code for block light, wind and ambient occlusion.
- Default render distance is up to 8 chunks. On an older machine, press **G** for Fast mode.
