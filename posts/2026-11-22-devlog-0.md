# Devlog #0: Starting over

There was an earlier version of this game. It's sitting on another machine behind an 8-hour transfer, which is close enough to "lost" that we're starting fresh.

Honestly? That's fine. A rebuild means cleaner code and a chance to make every piece *ours*.

## The goal

An infinite, block-built sandbox you can dig, build, and explore, running right in the browser. Familiar genre, original game: our own names, art, sounds, creatures, and at least one signature mechanic nobody else has.

## The plan

The full plan is on the [Roadmap](#/roadmap). The short version:

1. **Foundations**: this site, the tech stack, a render loop
2. **Terrain**: chunks, noise, fast meshing
3. **Player**: movement, collision, breaking and placing
4. **Persistence**: saves that survive
5. **Original content**: blocks, art, sound
6. **Living world**: day/night, creatures
7. **Making things**: inventory and crafting
8. **Polish and release**

## Lesson learned

Every session ends with a `git push`. No exceptions.
